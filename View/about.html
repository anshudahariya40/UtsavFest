The story behind UtsavFest

Where campus life becomes a celebration.

UtsavFest is the shared space for discovering the festivals, traditions, and unforgettable moments that bring our university community together.

About our university

Learning grows stronger when people celebrate together.

Columbia Professional University is a multidisciplinary campus in Chhattisgarh built around opportunity, practical learning, and community. Our colleges in Engineering, Pharmacy, Management, Nursing, and Teacher Education help students build knowledge with confidence.

From cultural festivals and music nights to student-led gatherings, our campus gives every voice a place. UtsavFest brings those experiences together so students can explore what is happening, find their people, and make memories beyond the classroom.
